Guide

How to read this page

What you see. A lithium extraction pilot on one skid: Dead Sea water from the pond → pump P-101 → filters 50 → 10 → 1 µm → UF → NMR-in analyser → valve battery → 8 columns C1–C8. Water, acid and NaCl come from pumps P-201, P-301 and P-401. The columns run as a carousel: each takes its turn through sorption → water wash → desorption → NaCl wash (pH 10, until the outlet is back to pH ≈ 7).

Pipe colour = medium: blue — brine, light blue — water wash, orange — acid, green — NaCl wash, yellow — eluate (product), grey — sulfate. A grey column on the chart means Hold (stopped on breakthrough).

Bottom bar: ▶/⏸ — run/pause the carousel · ×0.5/×1/×2 — speed (at ×1, one process hour = 20 s) · Transparent — see-through walls, flow visible inside pipes and columns · 🏷 — show/hide labels above the equipment · ◴ — auto-rotate · ⌂ — reset camera · Hide/Show — hide/bring back all panels · Help — this guide · Tour (or T) — a step-by-step guided tour that points at each control.

Viewing one step: there are no separate mode buttons — all steps run at once in different columns. Click a row in the Flow streams legend (right) or a bar in the Carousel chart (bottom right): that step's pipes light up and the rest dims. Click again to clear.

Mouse: drag — orbit · wheel — zoom · right-drag — pan · hover — unit name · click — details card.

Panels can be dragged. HMI (top left) — live readings: pumps, NMR in and out, breakthrough set-point, valves. Process snapshot — headline figures.
